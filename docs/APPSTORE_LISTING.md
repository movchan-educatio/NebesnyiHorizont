## 4. Privacy Policy Draft (App Store Connect & Website)

**Privacy Policy — Nebesnyi Horizont**
*Effective Date: November 17, 2026*

1. **No Personal Data Collection:**
   *Nebesnyi Horizont* does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, analytics, identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, camera feeds, or microphone audio.
2. **Local Storage Only:**
   All user preferences, controller calibrations, flight rates, and pilot progression records are stored strictly locally on your device.
3. **Local Network Usage (`NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription`):**
   The app requests Local Network access solely to receive real-time joystick channel packets (CRSF over UDP port `14550`) when the user optionally connects their device to a personal ExpressLRS Wi-Fi RC transmitter (`10.0.0.1`). No data is sent to external servers or the internet.
4. **Third-Party SDKs & Tracking:**
   The app contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or in-app purchases.
5. **Contact:**
   For support or privacy inquiries, please visit: `https://github.com/movchan-educatio/NebesnyiHorizont/issues`.

## 5. App Review Notes (For Apple App Store Reviewer)

- **No Account / Login Required:** All modes (Flight School, Training, Campaign TAC-01, Hangar, Settings) are immediately playable offline with on-screen Touch Controls.
- **Why Local Network Permission is Requested (`NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription`):**
  FPV pilots optionally connect a physical RC transmitter running ExpressLRS Wi-Fi firmware (`ExpressLRS TX` AP, local UDP port `14550`) to fly with physical sticks. The app works 100% with on-screen touch controls or standard MFi/Bluetooth gamepads without requiring an external radio; declining the Local Network prompt does not block any gameplay feature.
- **Intellectual Property & Trademarks:**
  All 3D models, UI strings, and textures are free of third-party hardware trademarks or real-world state insignia.
